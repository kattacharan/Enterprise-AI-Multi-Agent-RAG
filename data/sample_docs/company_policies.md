# Enterprise Human Resources & Workplace Policies

## 1. Remote Work and Work From Home (WFH) Guidelines
- Employees are permitted up to 3 days of remote work per week under the hybrid model.
- Core business hours are 09:30 AM to 05:30 PM (EST / Local Time), during which employees are expected to be reachable on Slack and email.
- High-speed internet access (>50 Mbps) and a dedicated home workspace are required for remote work approval.

## 2. Equipment & Home Office Stipend
- Every full-time employee is eligible for a one-time Home Office Equipment Stipend of up to $1,500.
- Eligible expenses include ergonomic office chairs, standing desks, external 4K monitors, webcams, noise-canceling headsets, and keyboard/mouse peripherals.
- Expense claims must be submitted via the internal Expensify portal within 45 calendar days of purchase, accompanied by itemized receipts.
- Standard corporate laptops (MacBook Pro M-series or Lenovo ThinkPad X1 Carbon) are provided directly by IT Operations.

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Entitlements
- Full-time employees receive 22 days of flexible PTO per calendar year, accrued monthly at 1.83 days.
- Employees may roll over a maximum of 5 unused PTO days into the following calendar year. Unused rollover days expire on March 31st.
- Parental Leave: 16 weeks of fully paid leave for primary caregivers and 8 weeks for secondary caregivers, available after 6 months of continuous service.
- Sick Leave: Up to 10 days of paid sick leave annually without requiring a doctor's certificate for absences under 3 consecutive days.

## 4. Professional Development & Learning Allowance
- Each engineer and employee has an annual learning budget of $2,000 for conferences, AWS/GCP certifications, O'Reilly subscriptions, and professional books.
- Prior approval from the engineering manager is required for conference travel and courses exceeding $500.
